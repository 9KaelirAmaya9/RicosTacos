Baloo 2 ExtraBold — Very close to "Taco Fiesta" commercial font. Rounded terminals, tall x-height, festive Latin character. The rounded bowl shapes closely match the sign's "O", "C", "R" letterforms. Best overall match to the sign.
CSS: font-family: 'Baloo 2', cursive; font-weight: 800;
