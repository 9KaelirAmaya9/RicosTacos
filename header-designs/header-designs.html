⚫ "Split Panel" — Logo Left, Nav Right
A compact, modern sticky-nav approach. The lime-green logo panel is pinned to the left with a red border and a decorative zigzag right edge (echoing the sign's serrated right border). Navigation and CTA buttons live in the dark right panel. Serape stripe runs across the top only. Best for a persistent sticky header that doesn't dominate the viewport.

🌙 "Hero Banner" — Dark Background, Glowing Logo Badge
A premium dark-mode hero header. The logo is presented as a floating lime-green badge (bordered in red) centered on a deep dark background with a subtle lime radial glow. Serape stripes frame the top and bottom. A tagline with golden accent text and two CTA buttons sit below the badge, followed by a slim nav strip. Best for a modern, upscale feel while keeping the brand's festive identity.
